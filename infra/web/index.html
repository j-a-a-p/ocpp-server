Why Charger

Everything an association needs to offer charging, nothing it does not

Installing chargers is the easy part. Splitting the costs, keeping the building's connection safe and answering every question at the general meeting is where it gets hard. Charger takes care of that part.

Fair, exact billing

Every kilowatt hour is assigned to the resident whose card started the session. No flat fees, no guessing, no residents paying for their neighbours.

Hardly any work for the board

Manage households per address, schedule move-ins and move-outs in advance, and download a yearly PDF report per household. Board members spend minutes instead of evenings.

Protects the shared connection

Charging profiles limit the current per charger, so a car park full of cars at 18:00 never overloads the building's connection.

Cheaper at night and in the weekend

Two rates are built in: a normal rate and a lower off-peak rate for nights and weekends. Residents see the rate that applies and are rewarded for charging at quiet times.

A card for every car

Residents charge with their own RFID card, and can name their cards: the family car, the company car, a visitor. A new card is added with one click.

Safe by design

Residents sign in with a one-time link by email, so there are no passwords to forget, share or leak. Only invited residents get access.

How it works

From the first charger to the yearly report

We connect your chargers to Charger. After that it works on its own.

Connect the chargers

Charger speaks OCPP, the standard language of charging stations. Point your chargers at Charger and they show up in the management portal, with their live status.

Invite the residents

The board adds residents by email address. They activate their account with one click and register their charging cards.

Charge and settle

Residents swipe their card and charge. Every session is recorded with its energy and cost, so the monthly and yearly overviews are ready when you need them.

Resident on- & offboarding

Built for residents moving in and out

In every building, residents move. Charger organises each address into households with start and end dates so charging access and billing always match who actually lives there.

Households with a main member

The first resident at an address is the main member who receives invoices and updates. Both the board and the main member can invite household members so partners or family charge with their own cards under one household.

Planned move-ins ahead of handover

Invite incoming residents before they get the keys with a mandatory start date. Signing in early shows a welcome page explaining how charging works and the exact date their access starts; charging unlocks automatically on that day.

Clean move-outs and separate bills

Terminate a residency with one end date and every household member's charging stops automatically after move-out. Former residents keep read-only access to their past sessions, and consecutive households in the same year receive separate PDF reports.

Rates

Reward charging at quiet times

Charger knows the difference between a busy Tuesday afternoon and a quiet Sunday. The off-peak rate applies in the whole weekend and every night, so residents who charge while the building is quiet pay less, and the connection stays relaxed.

Normal rateMonday to Friday, 07:00 to 21:00
Off-peak rateEvery night 21:00 to 07:00 and the whole weekend

The board sets both prices, and can plan a new price ahead of time. Times follow the clock in the Netherlands, also when the clocks change.

Questions

What boards usually ask

Which chargers does Charger work with?

Charger works with charging stations that support OCPP, the open standard that most chargers for shared parking use. If you have chargers already, we check them with you. If you are still choosing, we are happy to advise.

How do residents pay for what they charge?

Charger records the energy of every session and the rate that applied. Residents see their costs per month, and the board can download a yearly PDF report per household addressed to the main member — even when two households live at the same address in the same year, each gets its own clean statement.

Do residents need to install an app or create a password?

No. The resident portal works in any browser. Residents sign in with a one-time link that we email them, so there is no password to remember and nothing to install.

Can we prevent the building's connection from being overloaded?

Yes. With charging profiles the board limits the current that a charger may use. The power analytics show when your car park is busiest, so you can set limits based on facts.

What happens when a resident moves out or a new household moves in?

The board terminates the current residency with an end date, which automatically ends charging for the entire household after that date while keeping their past sessions available read-only. The board can immediately schedule the incoming household with a start date: new residents see a welcome page explaining how charging works and when they can begin, and charging unlocks automatically on their start date.

Can visitors charge too?

Yes. A visitor can be given a card of a resident, which the resident can name, for example "Visitor". The sessions of that card then show up with that resident, and are easy to recognise.
